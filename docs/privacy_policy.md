# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 30, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how the Mobile App Screenshot Generator ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our service.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our service is designed with your privacy in mind. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data or images that you upload. All processing of your screenshots is done locally in your web browser.

- **No Image Storage**: The images you upload are processed directly on your device and are never sent to our servers.
- **No Personal Data**: We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect any personal information such as your name, email address, or IP address.

## 2. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies for the sole purpose of analyzing service usage and improving the user experience. This data is collected in an aggregated and anonymous form and cannot be used to identify individual users.

## 3. Third-Party Services

This service does not integrate with any third-party services that would have access to your data.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
